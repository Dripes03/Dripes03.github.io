Project Overview

An architectural scene based on the real life location Palace on the Isle in Warsaw, Poland. This project was a collaboration between a the 3D artist Suzanna Kwidzinska and myself. I was responsible for giving the environment life through vfx and shader work. The art direction set by the artist was a romantic, realistic depiction of a nighttime scene in the palace gardens, with a focus on warm, atmospheric lighting and subtle, naturalistic effects. The goal was to create a moody and immersive environment that felt both magical and grounded in reality.
